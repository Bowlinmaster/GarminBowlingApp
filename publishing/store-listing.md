## Full Description

Bowling Stats is a focused ten-pin bowling score tracker for compatible Garmin
watches. Record each roll as you bowl, follow the running score through all ten
frames, and review your bowling history without reaching for your phone.

Features:

- Fast pin-count entry for every roll
- Automatic strike, spare, and tenth-frame scoring
- Live score previews before confirming a roll
- Game history stored locally on the watch
- Statistics across all saved games
- Series grouping for games completed during the same bowling session
- Series statistics and individual game scorecards
- Average, high/low game, and first-ball statistics
- Strike, spare, single-pin spare, and multi-pin spare conversion rates
- Clean-frame percentage and open-frame totals
- Made/attempt counts displayed alongside percentages
- Completion date and time for saved games
- Button controls, with touch support on compatible watches
- Device-specific layouts for supported Garmin models

Bowling Stats does not require an account or network connection. Game history
and settings remain on the watch unless the app is removed or its saved data is
cleared.

This release supports simple pin-count entry. Individual standing-pin selection
is planned for a future version.

## Recommended Screenshots

Upload clean simulator captures without window chrome, debug overlays, or safe
area guides. A useful order is:

1. New game and first-roll selector
2. Second roll with a potential spare
3. Tenth-frame bonus roll
4. Completed game
5. All Statistics
6. Saved series list or Series Statistics
7. Individual games list
8. Saved-game scorecard detail

Prefer a representative round AMOLED device for the primary screenshots, then
include a round MIP device or the rectangular Venu Sq 2 when additional images
help demonstrate compatibility.
