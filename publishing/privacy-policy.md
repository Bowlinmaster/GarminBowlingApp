# Bowling Stats Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 16, 2026

Bowling Stats is a Garmin Connect IQ application for recording ten-pin bowling
scores on a compatible Garmin device.

## Information Stored By The App

Bowling Stats stores bowling game results, completion dates and times, and app
settings locally in the application's private storage on the Garmin device.
This information is used only to provide scorekeeping and game-history features
inside the app.

## Information Collection And Sharing

Bowling Stats does not require an account, collect personal information,
include analytics or advertising, transmit saved games over a network, or share
app data with the developer or third parties.

## Data Retention And Deletion

Saved games remain on the device until the user clears saved games through the
app's settings, removes the application, or the device removes the app's stored
data. Bowling Stats has no remote copy of this information and cannot recover
deleted games.

## Garmin Services

Installation and distribution are provided through Garmin and may be subject
to Garmin's own privacy practices. Bowling Stats does not receive Garmin account
information from the Connect IQ Store.

## Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. Material
changes will be reflected in this document and its effective date.
